# Stances in the Algebra of Distinctions

**A brief on the FalseWork program for computational metaphysicians and formal philosophers**

*August 2026. This is the philosopher-facing front door; the lay version is `plain-brief.md`, the technical record is the papers and the Lean development. House discipline throughout: every claim is graded — **[K]** kernel-checked in Lean 4 against Mathlib4 (axiom audits: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only; no `sorry`); **[C]** classical result or established literature, cited; **[computed]** exhaustive finite computation under two-implementation agreement; **[A]** interpretive mapping, argued not proved; **[H]** falsifiable empirical hypothesis, untested; **[O]** open. The grades are load-bearing. Nothing below claims more than its tag. And two sentences of § 6 worth having before anything else: no claim is made that culture, perception, or knowledge IS an algebra — every domain mapping is a separately defended dictionary; and nothing here models the dynamics of inquiry. These are theorems about where structure lives across grains, not about how anyone moves between them.*

---

## 1. The move

FalseWork is an independent research program that formalizes how committed practices — musical, scientific, interpretive — organize around distinctions that cannot be eliminated, prototyping claims computationally, kernel-checking them where possible, and grading everything else. Its core move, stated for this audience:

Take the Heyting algebra — the algebra of intuitionistic logic, equivalently the subobject structure of a topos — not as a semantics for constructive proof but as an **ontology of distinctions**, and ask a question neither logic nor metaphysics has asked of it directly: *where in such a structure is it possible to take a stance?* (Terminological caution, since this audience owns the word: "stance" throughout means *position in a partition relative to a distinguished element*, not Dennett's predictive stances. The collision is unlucky and flagged here so it does no silent work.)

The motivating figure is the Pythagorean comma. A domain is founded on a distinction that fails to cancel — powers of 2 and powers of 3 never meet, so the circle of fifths never closes — and everything practitioners do is a way of standing toward the remainder. The program's wager is that this is the general shape of a committed practice: a **kernel** (the distinguished element around which a domain organizes) managing a residue that cannot be eliminated, only relocated. Readers who prefer a canonical philosophical object as the entry point should read § 2's second paragraph first: the entire stance apparatus turns out to be Aristotle's square of opposition, opened.

## 2. The proved spine

**The four-position partition [K].** Fix a Heyting algebra and a kernel element *a*. Every element falls into exactly one of four positions: **Infrastructure** (x ≤ a: working inside the commitment), **Refusal** (x ≤ ¬a: standing wholly outside it), **Exploitation** (x ≤ ¬¬a but x ⊄ a: occupying the double-negation residue — territory the commitment claims but does not settle; the name carries its cultural-analysis provenance, mining rather than malice), and **Distribution** (straddling both sides: carrying the commitment across its own boundary). Exhaustive, exclusive, machine-checked (`four_position_partition`). And the partition is *non-degenerate* — all four cells inhabited — precisely when *a* is **ordinary** in Citkin's sense: neither regular (¬¬a = a) nor dense (¬a = ⊥) (`allFourCellsInhabited_iff`, `partition_nondegenerate_iff_kernel_ordinary`). Position-taking has an exact algebraic precondition.

**The opened square [K].** The four positions are not an invented typology. Take Aristotle's square of opposition at *a*: it generates six landmarks (⊥, ¬a, a, ¬¬a, a ∨ ¬a, ⊤). Classically two collapse — ¬¬a = a and a ∨ ¬a = ⊤ — which is why the square can never exhibit a middle. Intuitionistically the figure opens, the six landmarks are pairwise distinct exactly iff *a* is ordinary (`oppositionFigure_injective_iff`), and the four middle landmarks inhabit the four cells one-for-one. The stance-space is what the square of opposition becomes when excluded middle is withheld.

**The sterility of classicality [K]/[C].** In a Boolean algebra every element is regular; hence nothing is ordinary; hence no stance-space exists anywhere in a classical world. This is trivial algebra with non-trivial metaphysical content: **wherever excluded middle holds globally, there is nowhere to stand.** Total resolution and position-taking exclude one another — not as a slogan but as a two-line consequence of a checked theorem. The reply this invites — *so much the worse for "stances"; the result is an artifact of an intuitionistic formalism chosen to produce it* — deserves its answer up front. Two, both graded: the four-position typology was abstracted from domain practice (temperament history and the program's cultural casework) *before* it was formalized, and the algebra reproduced it rather than defining it into existence [A]; and the landmarks are not bespoke — they are the corners of the oldest formal object in logic, surviving under one withheld assumption. The formalism does not smuggle the stance-space in; the trichotomy theorem *prices* it: the middle exists iff double negation and excluded middle both fail at the kernel, and nowhere else.

**The threshold and the weld, graded ply by ply.** No Heyting algebra with fewer than six elements contains an ordinary element (`ordinary_card_ge_six`) [K], and the six-element structure order-embeds into *every* algebra that contains one (`div12OrderEmbedding`) [K]. Precision matters here: it is an *order* embedding — the stronger claim, that the copy is a Heyting subalgebra, is provably **false** (the six-element image is not implication-closed in the free algebra on one generator; the program proved its own stronger conjecture wrong and kept the record). What is universal is the order-skeleton: the Hasse diagram of any stance-bearing world contains this six-element shape, though its implication structure need not restrict to it. That skeleton is, element for element, the subgroup lattice of ℤ/12 with the kernel at the order-2 subgroup (`Div12.one_generated_by_tritone`) [K] — and the kernel-checked content ends exactly there. Reading ℤ/12's subgroup lattice as the pitch-class lattice of twelve-tone equal temperament is the standard music-theoretic identification [C]; reading *that* as "the lattice of Western tonality" is this program's dictionary [A]. The weld is three-ply — [K] to the subgroups of ℤ/12, [C] to pitch classes, [A] to tonality — and its force survives the grading: the minimal stance-bearing order-shape did not have to be the lattice music had been living in for three centuries.

## 3. The observer layer

The 2026 development relativizes everything above to observers, and this is where the program believes it has found something genuinely new.

A **nucleus** — inflationary, idempotent, meet-preserving; the algebraic trace of a Lawvere–Tierney topology, i.e. of a sheafification — is a *lawful coarse-graining*, and its fix-set is again a Heyting algebra: **the world at that resolution** [C]. Define the **aperture** of a kernel as the set of nuclei under which its image remains ordinary *inside their world* — equivalently, by the bridge theorem, the set of observers whose world still exhibits a four-position stance-space around the distinction. The aperture is computable, and on divisor lattices it has a closed form via inclusion-exclusion over prime chains — kernel-checked on all two-prime lattices, exhaustively verified on 164 elements of fifteen algebras (`aperture_closed_form_exponents`) [K]/[computed].

Three results carry the philosophical weight:

**Fragility [K].** In the minimal stance-bearing world, the aperture of the unique kernel is the identity alone (`aperture_two_complete`, quantified over all nuclei). At the threshold of possibility, the stance-space survives *no* coarsening whatsoever. There exist distinctions whose position space is visible only to the observer who merges nothing.

**Latency [K] — the headline.** In the divisor lattice of 36 there is an element (6) that is *dense* at full resolution — no stance-space, provably, not as a matter of difficulty but of non-existence — which acquires a **complete** four-position space under exactly two proper nuclei, and no others (`latent_ordinariness_witness`, `aperture_six_complete`). Structure that is constitutively perspectival: absent at fine grain, present at specific coarse grains, with the set of revealing standpoints exactly countable. If you have wanted Dennett's "real patterns" question — *real at which levels of description, exactly?* — to have exact answers, this is a formally sharp *cousin* of that question, with closed-form counts. Cousin, not twin: real patterns are individuated by compressibility and predictive leverage, nucleus-worlds by lawful coarse-graining of a subobject lattice, and the identification is not made here. The characterization is complete on divisor lattices — latency occurs precisely when no prime is absent, some prime's exponent is strictly interior, and some *other* prime sits below its ceiling, so square-free worlds contain no perspectival structure at all [computed]. (An earlier form of this sentence said "every exponent strictly interior"; that rule is correct only at exactly two primes, and the correction — dated 2026-08-24, found by the program's own instrument in its own published dataset — is Result 6.3 of the aperture paper. The square-free consequence survives.) And genericity, since a careful reader will ask: of the 164 elements measured, 109 have empty apertures [computed]. Stance-space is the exception in the space of distinctions, and where apertures are nonempty the closed form prices them exactly. (This puts no knife-edge under the § 5 experiment, which targets designed material at an element whose aperture is known and kernel-checked.)

**The blind reductions [K]/[C].** The double-negation nucleus — Glivenko's Boolean pocket, the maximally reduced classical core of any Heyting algebra — opens *nothing*: its world is Boolean and Boolean worlds are stance-free. The fully compressed view is not approximately stance-free but exactly so. Reducibility and position-taking trade off as a theorem, which is the static shadow of the Wolfram-style intuition that interesting life happens outside the pockets of reducibility (the dynamical bridge is explicitly open, § 5).

## 4. The method is part of the position

Two features of the program's practice are themselves of metaphilosophical interest.

First, **the framework proves its own inapplicability conditions.** The four-position scheme is not a hermeneutic that fits anything: the trichotomy theorem states exactly when the partition degenerates (kernel regular or dense), so every application carries a built-in *not-valid-here* detector — as a theorem, not a promise. Interpretive frameworks that can be applied to everything can be wrong about nothing; this one is falsifiable at the point of application.

Second, **the grading discipline is enforced end to end.** The deductive spine is kernel-checked in Lean against Mathlib with clean axiom audits; finite claims are computed twice in independent implementations with predictions pre-registered before runs; interpretive mappings are tagged [A] and carry their disanalogies in the same breath (the papers state, with equal weight, where the observer analogy *fails*). Speculative metaphysics conducted at this grade of bookkeeping is rare enough that the practice may interest readers who reject every substantive claim.

## 5. The outward wagers, graded

**Hearing [H].** A companion hypothesis paper stakes the first empirical reading: settled auditory attention (metrical entrainment) acts on the lattice of metrical grids as a nucleus. The hypothesis decomposes into three independently falsifiable axioms — attention merges but never invents distinctions; a settled hearing is a fixed point; grouping commutes with combination — and yields a pre-registrable psychoacoustic test on the latency pair. Operationally: two-interval same/different discrimination on 36-pulse click cycles whose target and foil differ only in the level-6 structural relation; the measurable is per-condition discrimination accuracy, predicted above chance only under the two computed entrainments, at chance under full attention (the strong, counterintuitive prediction), at chance under a matched-depth control grouping, and at chance throughout on square-free 30-pulse control material. A wrong-in-which-axiom failure semantics is specified. Perspectival structure, if the wager holds, stops being a model of perception and becomes a measurement of it.

**The epistemic reading [A]/[O], in progress.** Nucleus as grain of description; the theorems as epistemology: standpoints form a finite lawful census; some commitments are legible only at maximal grain; some structure is constitutively coarse-grained — the exact model that perspectival realism has argued for and lacked (Giere, *Scientific Perspectivism*, 2006; Massimi, *Perspectival Realism*, 2022): perspectival without being any less real; and the fully classical standpoint sees nothing worth standing for. (Standpoint epistemology's distinctive claims — the achievement thesis, the epistemic privilege of particular social positions — are *not* what this formalizes, and the program does not borrow their authority.) The planned test is case formalization with pre-registered structure — statistical mechanics as the canonical instance, temperature as the real world's latent element.

**The Wolfram bridge [A]/[O].** Nuclei model *static* resolution; computational irreducibility is a claim about *temporal cost*. The rhyme is strong (reduced pockets are stance-free on both pictures), the identification is not made, and whether nuclei can be induced dynamically — by the equivalences a bounded observer can afford to compute — is stated as an open problem, not a result.

**The first field deployment [computed], delivered.** In August 2026 the aperture ran as an instrument on a corpus outside the program's control: Mathlib's import structure (eventually all twenty-one namespaces, ~7,900 modules; `mathlib-study/`, eighteen scripts, every test pre-registered in the script headers before first run). Findings, briefly: real dependency cones are narrow-aperture (≈18× narrower than degree-preserving randomizations, unexplained by four simple graph invariants); latency exists in the wild (11 of 18 principal kernels on the pre-registered cone, all 262,144 worlds enumerated per kernel); and six pre-registered consolidation trends across three years of git history all point the same way — latency rising, apertures narrowing. The control gap that sentence originally carried (no per-snapshot null) was closed on 2026-08-26 with the outcome registered in advance: the arrow survives per-snapshot degree-preserving nulls 6/6 — and the sharper fact is that the 2023 library sat *inside* its own null envelope while the 2026 library sits at the extremes (100th percentile on latency, 0–3rd on aperture, all three namespaces). Consolidation is a measured departure from degree structure, not a byproduct of growth. The record keeps two operator predictions the instrument overruled and one hypothesis killed by its own pre-registered test. Citable snapshot: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22016585.

**The cell dictionary, tested where it is most checkable [computed] — and taken through full out-of-sample confirmation.** The four positions' *names* are an [A]-graded dictionary, and Mathlib is the one corpus where role ground truth is cheap enough to test them. A pre-registered study (2026-08-26) compared the cells' composition against the human conceptual taxonomy in module name paths, under a name-permutation null that preserves all import structure and cell sizes; its positive half was then sent, as a registered prediction, into thirteen held-out namespaces it had never seen, and the corpus was exhausted the same day (sixteen scripts; all twenty-one namespaces used). The verdict that survived everything: **Exploitation-cell modules really do occupy the kernel's named territory** — name-closer to the apex far beyond what chance placement allows, in 16 of 16 registered namespaces (20 of 21 counting descriptive runs), zero significant reversals — corpus-level empirical support for a contentful cell reading, and for exactly the cell that exists only non-classically. The Refusal and Distribution *spatial* glosses did not survive: the original ordering failed on the first three namespaces; the appealing corrected reading ("refusal happens on-territory") was registered out-of-sample and refuted by significant reversal in 8 of 13; and the original ordering, scored on the final five fresh namespaces, failed there too. Where those two cells' occupants live relative to the kernel's named territory is namespace-contingent — suggestively, the refusal-proximate namespaces are Mathlib's foundational strata (Order, Topology, Logic, SetTheory), but the corpus is spent and that reading waits for another one. The proved cell *definitions* are untouched; what died is geography. Two instrument deaths are on the books beside these (a saturated statistic whose null could not move, caught by the program's own diagnostic; and the corrected reading, killed within the day it was proposed).

**The referendum [computed]: a second ecosystem, same day.** Because every empirical finding above rested on one corpus, the program staged its own decision point on the Archive of Formal Proofs — a different proof assistant (Isabelle), community, grain (1,014 refereed entries), ground truth (the archive's curated topic taxonomy), and twenty-two years of history (`afp-study/`, seven scripts, blind pre-checks committed first). Verdict, both halves registered in advance: **Exploitation-on-territory graduates** — it holds on the AFP entry graph at the 100th percentile of its topic-permutation null, and descriptively at the 100th percentile inside every stratum; the finding is now a cross-ecosystem regularity of collective formal work, not a Mathlib fact. **The consolidation arrow does not transfer**: over eight biennial checkpoints latency rises (Spearman +0.83, as on Mathlib) but apertures *widen* (+0.64 where the registered prediction required ≤ −0.6), so the arrow is scoped to Mathlib wherever it is cited. A flagged post-hoc reading with its own testable content: Mathlib is a *maintained* library, continuously refactored; AFP is a *frozen* archive, never rewired after acceptance — consolidation may be what maintenance looks like, and absence-of-consolidation what archiving looks like. The foundational-strata reading of R/D geography, registered here after surfacing post hoc on Mathlib, failed too — that geography is now dead on every corpus it has been scored on.

**The growth studies [computed]: the axis acquires a second, independent fingerprint.** The program's first *dynamical* prediction — that Exploitation-cell members, sitting on claimed-but-unsettled territory, disproportionately become load-bearing later — was registered on AFP's history with a degree-matched, within-kernel design and a label-permutation null built to kill the preferential-attachment confound. It failed by significant reversal: at matched degree, **Distribution**-cell members are the ones that grow (percentile 0 of the null; ordering D > E > R). The registered replication on Mathlib's history, with the flagged age confound matched away, then reversed in the *opposite* direction: there **Exploitation** grows (percentile 100; E > D > R). Neither corpus behaves as predicted, and they disagree with each other — but they disagree along the same line the consolidation split drew: the maintained library grows through its residue cell, the frozen archive through its boundary-straddling cell. One axis now separates the two corpora on two independent measurements (consolidation direction and growth-cell identity), which is what promotes the maintenance-regime reading from post-hoc consolation to the program's most motivated open hypothesis [H]. It remains exactly that: two corpora are a line through two points, and the discriminating test — a maintained/archival pair from a different domain entirely (the Go standard library against the crates.io registry, whose published versions are immutable by platform policy), with a manipulation check gating all scoring and four-quadrant predictions — is registered as an unrun protocol, committed before any data acquisition (`software-study/PROTOCOL.md`). Anyone may execute it.

**The second field deployment [computed], delivered — a clean negative, earned the hard way.** Same month (`ca-study/`): the aperture on the counterfactual causal graphs of a running cellular automaton (Conway's Life), the first substrate where nothing was authored or curated by anyone. Four protocol versions, three killed by the program's own discipline: the first died by inspection before any run (its causal relation was provably state-independent — the pre-registration is committed unexecuted, with the proof as its postscript); the second died by its own stop rule with a two-line structural cause; the third, depth-matched version found the pooled negative (coherent patterns statistically indistinguishable from random soup) plus one apparent positive — still-life cones running ~17× wider than soup (p = 0.044) — reported with its cone-size entanglement named and a size-controlled follow-up required before graduation. The fourth version was that follow-up, registered with the deflationary prior disclosed as better-supported, and it killed the lead within hours of its being reported: at matched cone size, still lifes and soup are indistinguishable (p = 0.364; every smallest-stratum cone in the study, still life or soup, is a single structural class), and local quiescence carries nothing (p = 1.0). The apparent signal was a size law (median aperture fraction falling 0.098 → 0.016 → 0.0075 as cone size grows 9 → 16 → 23), now on the record as the substrate's one clean descriptive fact. What stands: the Mathlib narrowness result does **not** transfer (real cones sit inside their own degree-matched null distributions), scoping the ≈18× to curated dependency structures specifically; and latency is *generic* there (67–94% of kernels in every non-trivial cone), completing a cross-substrate map — full-resolution stance-space rare on divisor lattices, common on Mathlib, rare on Life cones, while structure-visible-to-*some*-lawful-observer is the rule in the wild everywhere it has been measured. On this substrate, under this construction, the aperture measures cone size and isomorphism class, not what the pattern is — stated at the same prominence any positive would have received.

## 6. What is not claimed

- Not that culture, perception, or knowledge *is* a Heyting algebra. Every domain mapping (works to elements, hearings to nuclei, grains to nuclei) is a separately defended [A] dictionary, and the program's own measurements show position assignments are instrument-dependent (Paper 2 § 3.7: 21% alignment between a classifier and a score-feature proxy on the Bach chorale corpus) [computed] — a finding the aperture theorems now explain rather than merely record.
- Not a dynamics. Nothing here models inquiry's time, cost, or belief revision. Where things live across grains, not how anyone moves between them.
- Not priority beyond a search. The parent literatures (nuclei; ordinary elements) are classical, and the program's own citation hunt (2026-08) located the closest classical result and credits it: the classification of nuclei on finite down-set algebras — one per sub-poset, 2^n in all — is Simmons (1980), with the poset form in Bezhanishvili et al. (Order, 2020) [C]. Neither touches the layer built on top: ordinariness relativized to nucleus worlds, the aperture invariant, latency, the counting formula. That intersection still appears unvisited, now on a deliberate search rather than a passive one [O]. Correction is welcome and would be recorded.

## 7. Why this might be worth your afternoon

Because it holds theorems where the genre holds gestures. "The classical world is stance-free," "some structure exists only from certain standpoints, and here is the exact count of them," "the minimal world in which position-taking is possible has the order-skeleton of the twelve-tone pitch-class lattice, and its stance-space dies under every possible blur" — each of these sentences, which would be programmatic hand-waving in a metaphysics paper, is here the [A]-graded reading of a specific machine-checked proof object you can go and audit. The interesting disagreements this program invites are therefore unusually well-posed: you can dispute a dictionary while conceding a theorem, or demand a grade be lowered, and the papers are built so that either move lands somewhere exact.
