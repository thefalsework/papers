## 0. One breath

> **A domain is a distinction that fails to cancel; practice is the four-fold of relations to that failure; history is those relations nesting into new distinctions.**

That sentence is **[A]** — an ontological gloss of the whole programme, not a theorem. Everything below unpacks it. What *is* theorem is the middle clause's formal content: once a productive mark leaves a residue (a *kernel*), every subsequent act falls into exactly one of four relations to that residue, and under the right conditions those four relations are the same four middle landmarks Aristotle's square of opposition generates when the ambient logic stops being classical.

If you remember only three further facts:

1. **The fourth position exists only because the mark does not heal.** Classical / Boolean logic collapses it. Exploitation is the scar of incomplete distinction, made into a place. **[K]**
2. **Music is where the story becomes literal.** The pitch-class lattice of twelve-tone equal temperament *is* the smallest algebra in which all four positions open, and the tritone *is* the unique kernel. **[K]** Working kernel and formal kernel coincide there; elsewhere they may come apart.
3. **The placements of works are interpretations.** The cells are theorems; where Bach or Beckett sits is a reading. The project exists partly to keep that seam visible (Paper 2).

## 1. The claim

Every domain of sustained practice organized around a minimal generative operation encounters a structural limit it cannot resolve from inside its own rules. Paper 1 calls the operation a **kernel** and the limit a **comma** (after the Pythagorean comma in music — the gap left when twelve perfect fifths fail to close the octave).

The underlying claim, in Paper 1's own one-sentence form: *in any field of practice, an irreducible productive asymmetry forces every act in the field to occupy a position in a structured space of resolution strategies generated by the asymmetry itself.*

Two senses of "kernel," kept honest:

| Sense | What it means | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Working kernel** | The inherited generative constraint a domain cannot escape (Paper 1's four prose criteria: prior, monogenic, inescapable, self-limiting) | Proposed structure for practice domains **[A]**; music independently corroborated in part |
| **Formal kernel** | An *ordinary* element of a Heyting algebra — neither regular (`¬¬k = k`) nor dense (`¬k = ⊥`) — relative to which the four-position partition is non-degenerate | Theorem **[K]** |

In music they **provably coincide** (monogenic ↔ one-generated by the tritone; self-limiting ↔ `¬¬k ≠ k`; inescapable ↔ every element is a term in the generator). Elsewhere they can decouple: there exist algebras that are self-limiting yet not monogenic (`H8`, `unique_ordinary_converse_false` **[K]**). So a domain may have a working kernel with no formal kernel established behind it. Literature, in the programme's own reading, is exactly that situation. The universal claim of Paper 1 rides the *working* kernel; only music currently rides both.

## 2. The floor: a distinction that fails to cancel

Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form* (1969) founds a calculus on drawing a distinction, with two axioms: **calling** (marking twice is marking once) and **crossing** (marking and unmarking cancel). FalseWork's kernel object is the categorification of the first *without* forcing the second:

- A **distinction structure** is an endofunctor `D` with a marking unit `η` and an idempotency witness `D ∘ D ≅ D` — calling, at the natural-transformation level. **[K]** `DistinctionStructure`
- When crossing holds — when the ambient logic is Boolean — the Exploitation cell is empty and the partition sees three cells, not four. **[K]** `boolean_partition_three_cells`, `exploitation_requires_nonBoolean`

So the programme's founding move is not "add more truth values" (the usual *Laws of Form* extensions). It is: **weaken negation to the Heyting case and keep the scar.** The asymmetry Paper 1 talks about *is* the failure of the mark to cancel — non-regularity, `¬¬k ≠ k`, the comma made formal.

Beneath that: Cantor's diagonal and Lawvere's fixed-point theorem as the set-theoretic / categorical floor of "a system enumerating its own distinctions generates an excess it cannot contain" (Paper 3; `MathFloorCantor.lean` **[K]** for the checked instances). Paper 4's Level 0 — the unmarked process prior to any distinction — is the speculative ontological commitment, graded **[A]** throughout.

## 3. The four-fold (and why classical eyes miss one)

Fix a kernel `k`. Every act (formally: every non-trivial image relative to `k`) falls into exactly one cell:

| Position | Formal shape | Plain gloss **[A]** | Opened-square landmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Infrastructure** | `x ≤ k` | Lives inside the kernel as transparent ground | **A** = `k` |
| **Refusal** | `x ≤ ¬k` | Structured against the kernel; negation is the argument | **E** = `¬k` |
| **Exploitation** | `x ≤ ¬¬k` and not `x ≤ k` | Inhabits the residue the kernel cannot reach; the limit is the material | **I** = `¬¬k` |
| **Distribution** | meets both `k` and `¬k` non-trivially | Spreads the gap across sites so no single wall bears it all | **U** = `k ∨ ¬k` |

**Commitment** is not a fifth cell. It is a binary fidelity gate *within* a cell: does the work pursue that cell's logic to the cell's structural limit? **[A]** for the cultural reading; schema-grade formally (the attempt to unify the four predicates as one theorem closed negative, 2026-05-10).

Three load-bearing facts, all **[K]**:

- **Partition.** The four cells are disjoint and exhaustive (`four_position_partition`).
- **Non-degeneracy = ordinariness.** All four cells are available at `k` iff `k` is ordinary — neither regular nor dense (`allFourCellsInhabited_iff`; bridge: `partition_nondegenerate_iff_kernel_ordinary`).
- **Opened square.** Aristotle's square, read at `k`, generates six landmarks `⊥, ¬k, k, ¬¬k, k∨¬k, ⊤`. Classically two collapse. Intuitionistically they are pairwise distinct **iff** `k` is ordinary — the same condition — and the four middle landmarks inhabit the four cells one-for-one (`oppositionFigure_injective_iff`, `oppositionFigure_skeleton`). The square's 2,300-year silence about the extra positions was a theorem about classical logic, not an oversight.

Two structural laws the interpretive layer inherits from the algebra (predicates **[K]**, cultural force **[A]**):

1. **Exploitation is classically unstatable.** When `¬¬k = k`, the cell vanishes into Infrastructure. A classical vocabulary cannot even name the position.
2. **Refusal is kernel-bound, not kernel-free.** `¬k` is as far from the kernel as you can get while remaining defined by it. You do not leave a domain by refusing it; refusal is the most charged way of staying. You exit by *indifference* — when the kernel ceases to matter — not by intensity of rejection.

## 4. Why twelve: the forced music instance

Among equal temperaments ℤ/n, a unique formal kernel exists (under the checked scope) iff `n = p²q`, and the least such `n` is **12**, kernel the **tritone**. **[K]** `why_twelve`, `twelve_kernel_unique`. Independently: the smallest Heyting algebra with an ordinary element has six elements; that algebra `Z₆` *is* the divisor / subgroup lattice of 12; at the tritone the opened square is the identity — the six landmarks *are* the six elements. **[K]**

| Landmark | Heyting | Pitch-class witness | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `k` | tritone ⟨6⟩ | Infrastructure |
| E | `¬k` | augmented triad ⟨4⟩ | Refusal |
| I | `¬¬k` | diminished seventh ⟨3⟩ | Exploitation |
| U | `k ∨ ¬k` | whole-tone ⟨2⟩ | Distribution |

This is why music is the programme's load-bearing example, not a decorative analogy: the Diophantine comma (fifths never close octaves), the Heyting non-regularity of the tritone, and the four-position partition meet in one lattice. Paper 5 and `connecting-the-spine.md` weld the arithmetic and logic registers; the comma-*functor* identifying them as one constructed map remains **[O]**.

The deep specimen is [`bach-at-the-kernel.md`](bach-at-the-kernel.md): Bach in three cells (chorales → Infrastructure; WTC → Distribution; dim7 practice → Exploitation), Refusal empty until the later whole-tone tradition, *Es ist genug* walked up the entire stack. That essay is the music chapter of this guide; this guide is the city around it.

## 6. History as nesting

The third clause of §0 is the programme's account of tradition without sociology-first storytelling.

A distinction leaves a residue; practitioners take positions relative to it; some commitment-grade acts *found new distinctions* that later works relate to. Formally, the handle is **canonization closure**: an idempotent monad induced by a commitment-grade act, yielding a new distinction structure and thus a new four-fold (`CanonizationClosure.lean`, recursive partition **[K]** for the abstract composition; which historical figures so function is **[A]**).

Worked readings:

- Bach as Infrastructure occupant *and* as generator of the tonal-pedagogy region later works inhabit
- Bach states the tritone inside Infrastructure; Berg restates it inside Exploitation — same landmark, later distinction
- Paper 6 (exploratory): canonical status as the trace of confrontation with an open comma — not resolution of the comma, but sustained contact with it **[A]**

Russian Ark, in the corrected four-cell dictionary: not a fifth "Commitment" position, but **(Exploitation, commitment-yes)** — the cut's continuity-management closure worked past the standard stopping point. The gate marks fidelity within a cell; it does not add a cell.

## 7. Method: how the programme stays honest

Paper 2 is the series' conscience. Its thesis, compressed: *epistemic dependency is structural in every knowledge system; what differs is the maturity of correction.*

The operational discipline is the tag ladder:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[K]** | Kernel-checked in Lean / Mathlib; axiom audit disclosed |
| **[C]** | Classical mathematics, cited not re-proved |
| **[A]** | Structural analogy / interpretive claim |
| **[O]** | Open; specialist-gated or unbuilt |

This field guide is mostly [A] with [K] floorboards. Paper 2 documents AI-assisted scholarship as a case study of dependency *and* correction — including literature engagement (Citkin), institutional boundary (Mathlib AI policy), and the rule that model recall is not a source. The companion note `memory-is-not-a-source.md` isolates the sharpest transferable rule: a finite oracle is a sound *refuter* but an unsound *confirmer*.

Without this section the totality looks like a system that always agrees with itself. With it, the totality includes the record of being wrong and correcting.

## 10. What this guide is not claiming

- Not that the ontological sentence of §0 is a theorem.
- Not that every domain has a formal ordinary kernel.
- Not that the placements of composers and writers are classifications forced by Lean.
- Not that the programme explains aesthetic quality, greatness, or affect — it partitions relations to an obstruction; it is silent on worth.
- Not that Papers 1–6 have been superseded. This is a map; they are the territory.
- The kernel-checked claims cited by name above are exactly those claims. Everything else dies without taking them with it.

## Closing

The totality is easier to grasp once you stop trying to hold every file and hold the pattern instead:

**Draw a mark that sticks and does not cancel. A residue remains. Every act in the field is typed by its relation to that residue — inside, against, in the shadow, or straddling. History is those types nesting into new marks. Classical logic could not see the shadow; the opened square is what the oldest diagram in logic becomes when the mark is allowed to stay open. Music is where the pattern is forced into a lattice you can hear. The rest of the corpus is that pattern argued, checked, graded, and applied — and corrected when the checking fails.**

That is FalseWork. The doors out are in §9.
