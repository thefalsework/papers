How to read it

A nucleus (observer) here is any subset of states it can resolve; coarser observer = smaller subset. Phantom mass counts the worlds it files under the kernel's name — confusion manufactured, not detail deleted. Aperture counts the observers for which the kernel still splits the world four ways. The seed state was dropped from the window deliberately: with a single origin included, theorem H1 makes every cone dense and the whole picture sterile — one more way the math says no observer gets to stand at the beginning and see everything. The phantom counter demonstrates the second law (S1) live: it never decreases as you coarsen, and equals exactly 1 at the identity observer (S2).
