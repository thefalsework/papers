## What is being claimed (and at what strength)

This is the math-anchor analogue of the music anchor's Diophantine floor. It records the **floor only**: the obstruction the domain hands over for free, plus its unification, kernel-checked. It does **not** claim a mathematics instantiation of the four-position partition — that is the deferred, pre-registered Phases 2–4.

### Kernel-checked (in-repo) — strength [K]

1. **The diagonal residue.** For any `f : α → Set α` and any `a : α`, the diagonal set `{x | x ∉ f x}` differs from `f a`. The diagonal is the residue: what a self-referential indexing generates but cannot contain — the Level-0 comma in set-theoretic form. *Axiom-free.*
   - Lean: `FalseWork.MathFloor.diagonal_escapes` (`Examples/MathFloorCantor.lean`).

2. **The floor (free).** No `f : α → Set α` is surjective. This is Mathlib's `Function.cantor_surjective`; the math floor is handed over by the library exactly as `irrational_sqrt_two` gave the music floor its rank-1 obstruction.
   - Lean: `FalseWork.MathFloor.cantor_no_surjection`.

3. **The unification (Lawvere).** A surjection `e : A → (A → B)` forces every `f : B → B` to have a fixed point. Cantor/Russell/Tarski/Gödel are the contrapositive instances where `B` carries a *fixed-point-free* operator. Cantor is `B = Bool`, `f = not` (`cantor_bool_via_lawvere`); Russell is `diagonal_escapes`. *`lawvere_fixedPoint` is axiom-free.*
   - Lean: `FalseWork.MathFloor.lawvere_fixedPoint`, `cantor_bool_via_lawvere`, bundled as `mathematics_floor`.

### Structural identification — strength [A] (prose, not theorem)

The reading "diagonal escape = FalseWork comma" is a structural identification, exactly as "Pythagorean near-miss = comma" was in the music floor. The theorems are `[K]`; the interpretation is `[A]`-adjacent and lives in prose. Lawvere makes the *unification* of Paper 4 §10 rigorous-adjacent — the common core is genuinely one lemma — but **Tarski (truth) and Gödel (provability) proper require a formalized truth/provability predicate** (the external `FormalizedFormalLogic` Foundation project) and are **`[O]`, named but not imported**.

### Phase 2–4 results (run 2026-06-09, kernel-checked) — strength [K]

The pre-registration below was written before any enumeration ran; the experiment was then run in `Examples/NishimuraTruncations.lean`. **Result: outcome (A)**, with one sharpening and one bonus.

1. **The truncations are canonical, dissolving the pre-registered worry (D).** By Citkin (2024, *Logics* 2(4), Thms 6 & 8) **[C]**, for every `n > 1` there is exactly one one-generated Heyting algebra `Z_n` of cardinality `n` (defined by the relation `x_n = 1` in the Nishimura terms). So "which truncation" is a single integer, and stability is well-posed. Each Lean algebra is certified as `Z_n` by cardinality + a kernel-checked one-generation theorem (every element an explicit Heyting term in `g`).

2. **Outcome (A): unique, truncation-stable forced kernel = the free generator.** At `n = 6, 7, 8`: `AllFourCellsInhabited a ↔ a = g`, kernel-checked (`Z7.kernel_unique`, `Z8.kernel_unique`, `Div12.kernel_unique`, bundled as `rn_truncation_outcome_A`; axioms `propext, Quot.sound` only). The four cells are inhabited by the *same* Nishimura terms at every level: Infrastructure `g`, Refusal `¬g`, Exploitation `¬¬g`, Distribution `g ⊔ ¬g`. The Exploitation witness is literally the double-negation non-collapse `¬¬g ≠ g` of intuitionistic logic.

3. **Sharpening: below cardinality 6 the partition is degenerate.** `Z5.no_kernel`: no element of `Z_5` (and a fortiori `Z_2`–`Z_4`, generated by regular/dense elements) makes all four cells inhabited. Cardinality 6 is the smallest stage of the free Heyting algebra where the four-position structure exists.

4. **Bonus: `Div12 ≅ Z_6` — the music lattice is the 6-element truncation.** `Div12.one_generated_by_tritone` **[K]**: every element of the music lattice is a Heyting term in the tritone (`¬two = three`, `¬¬two = four`, `two ⊔ ¬two = six`, `x₆ = twelve`). With `|Div12| = 6` and Citkin's uniqueness **[C]**, the music substrate *is* `Z_6` and the tritone *is* the free generator. The music witness cells (tritone, augmented triad, diminished seventh, whole-tone) coincide element-for-element with the logical terms (`p, ¬p, ¬¬p, p∨¬p`). Also upgraded: tritone kernel uniqueness in `Div12`, previously Wolfram-verified **[C]**, is now **[K]** (`Div12.kernel_unique`).

### The all-n kernel law (run 2026-06-09, kernel-checked) — strength [K] conditional on [C]

`Examples/NishimuraKernelLaw.lean` upgrades outcome (A) from three checked sizes to a law for the whole one-generated family, **without building any further finite algebras**. Two theorems:

1. **Kernel trichotomy (general, unconditional [K]).** `allFourCellsInhabited_iff`: in *any* Heyting algebra, all four cells are inhabited at `a` iff `a ≠ ⊥ ∧ ¬a ≠ ⊥ ∧ ¬¬a ≠ a` — the kernel must be non-zero, non-polar, and non-regular. Axiom audit: `propext` only. This retroactively explains every earlier instance: Boolean algebras have no non-degenerate kernel because every element is regular; `Z_5` has none because each of its five elements fails one of the three conditions. **Standard-terminology identification (added 2026-06-10, from reading Citkin 2024 §2.1):** the condition says exactly that `a` is *ordinary* (neither regular nor dense) — a standard notion, and Citkin's own term. Consequently (i) the partition is non-degenerate precisely at the ordinary elements; (ii) the `Z_2`–`Z_5` half of the threshold is *already observed in Citkin's paper* ("the algebras `Z_i`, `i ∈ [2,5]`, do not contain any ordinary elements"), so `Z5.no_kernel` is a kernel-checked re-derivation of a known observation, **not** a new result; (iii) what remains apparently new is the *uniqueness* of the ordinary element (point 2 below) and the weld/why-12 identifications — exactly the questions in the prior-art email (`docs/outreach/citkin-email.md`).

2. **The law (conditional [K]).** `nishimura_kernel_unique`: if every element of `H` is a Nishimura term value in `g` (hypothesis `hgen`, stated explicitly) and `g` is ordinary (`¬g ≠ ⊥`, `¬¬g ≠ g`), then the **unique** four-cell kernel of `H` is `g`. Proof: `x₀ = ⊥` fails non-zeroness; `x₁ = ¬g` and `x₃ = ¬¬g` are regular by the triple-negation law; every `xₙ` with `n ≥ 4` lies above `x₄ = ¬g ⊔ g` (`four_le_nishimuraTerm`, strong induction on the term recursion), hence has `⊥` complement; only `x₂ = g` survives. Axiom audit: `propext, Quot.sound`.

**Where [C] enters, exactly:** the conditional theorem is kernel-checked with `hgen` explicit, so nothing is borrowed. By Nishimura's theorem (Nishimura 1960; Citkin 2024) **[C]**, `hgen` holds for *every* one-generated Heyting algebra — so the law covers every `Z_n` (`n ≥ 6`) **and the full infinite Rieger–Nishimura lattice `F(1)`**: the free Heyting algebra on one generator has the free generator as its unique four-cell kernel. The [C] citation enters only when discharging `hgen` for a particular algebra; the Nishimura enumeration itself is not formalized.

**Consistency weld:** for `Div12 = Z_6` the hypothesis is discharged *inside Lean* (`Div12.nishimura_generated`, by `decide` — no [C] needed at cardinality 6), and `Div12.kernel_unique_via_law` re-derives the tritone-uniqueness result from the abstract law. Two independent proofs of the same statement — one exhaustive, one structural — agree.

### Pre-registration (written before the run; retained verbatim for the record)

The substrate for a mathematics *instantiation* of the partition would be the **Rieger–Nishimura lattice** (the free Heyting algebra on one generator). Two caveats are fixed in advance so the result cannot drift:

- **This is intuitionistic propositional logic, not "mathematics."** The substrate encodes *intuitionistic* logic on *one* atom; both are choices a classical mathematician may reject. There is **no encoding gap eliminated** — it relocates to "why intuitionistic Heyting on one generator = mathematics." Any anchor here is about intuitionistic propositional logic, stated as such.
- **One generator is *simplest*, not *forced*.** `F(1)`, `F(2)`, `F(ω)` are different lattices; the domain does not force one generator the way 12-tone temperament forced `ℤ/12`.

**Pre-registered Phase-3 outcomes** (forced-kernel experiment on finite truncations `RN n`, since `decide` cannot exhaust an infinite type), all acceptable, the kernel deciding:

- **(A)** Unique forced kernel, **stable** across truncations → the math "tritone"; novel small result.
- **(B)** Multiple kernels work, stably → real but less rigid than music; report the asymmetry.
- **(C)** No kernel works on any truncation → negative result; a constraint on the cross-domain claim.
- **(D)** Forced kernel is **truncation-dependent** (does not stabilize) → no canonical kernel; the "free object" selling point fails even though each `decide` passes. **Most likely failure mode**; a real result, not a bug to patch.

A finite truncation is itself a choice, so (A)/(B) are claimable only if **stable** in the truncation index. *(Post-run note: Citkin's uniqueness theorem made the truncation parameter canonical, which is why (D) could be ruled out cleanly rather than haunting the result.)*
