# The ladder-core program: universal threshold, order-embedded music lattice, H8 anatomy

**Status:** pre-registered 2026-06-11, before the Lean run. Results section to be filled after the build.
**Trigger:** Citkin's December 2025 preprint (arXiv:2512.05633, "Hereditarily Structurally Complete Superintuitionistic Logics and Primitive Varieties of Heyting Algebras"), read 2026-06-11 after his prior-art reply. Its Proposition 3.1 — *any Heyting algebra containing an ordinary element contains a subalgebra isomorphic to `Z_n`, `n ≥ 6`* — welded to our trichotomy (`allFourCellsInhabited_iff`) reads: every Heyting algebra with a non-degenerate four-cell kernel contains the Rieger–Nishimura ladder as a subalgebra. This claim file pre-registers the kernel-checkable fragment of that weld and its instance computations.

## Pre-registration (written before the run)

### Targets, with expected outcomes

1. **Universal six-element threshold [K target]** (`ordinary_card_ge_six`, `allFourCells_card_ge_six`). In *any* Heyting algebra with an ordinary element `g`, the six elements `⊥, g, ¬g, ¬¬g, g ⊔ ¬g, ⊤` are pairwise distinct; hence any finite Heyting algebra with an ordinary element — equivalently (trichotomy) with a non-degenerate four-cell kernel — has cardinality ≥ 6. **This is stronger than the prior art on both sides**: Citkin's Prop. 4(c) (2024) bounds *one-generated* algebras with ordinary *generators*; the target is any ordinary element in any Heyting algebra, with an abstract proof (no enumeration, no [C]). Expected: provable from pure Heyting reasoning (the load-bearing case is `g ⊔ ¬g ≠ ⊤`, via distributivity: `g ⊔ ¬g = ⊤` forces `¬¬g = g`). `Z5.no_kernel` becomes a corollary of a universal law rather than an exhaustive check.
2. **The music lattice order-embeds into every non-degenerate instance [K target]** (`div12_orderEmbedding_of_ordinary : IsOrdinary g → Div12 ↪o H`). The six elements above, with `one ↦ ⊥, two ↦ g, three ↦ ¬g, four ↦ ¬¬g, six ↦ g ⊔ ¬g, twelve ↦ ⊤`, realize the exact Hasse structure of `Div12 = Z_6` — including the two incomparabilities (`¬g ∥ ¬¬g`, `¬¬g ∥ g ⊔ ¬g`). **Scope guard, fixed in advance:** this is an *order*-embedding, NOT a Heyting-subalgebra embedding. The set need not be closed under `⇨` (in `Z_7`, `¬¬g ⊔ (g ⊔ ¬g)` is a seventh element). The subalgebra version is exactly Citkin's Prop. 3.1 and requires the Nishimura normal-form theorem — pre-registered below as [O], not claimed here.
3. **H8 anatomy: the ladder core is `Z_7` [K target]** (`z7ToH8` embedding). The subalgebra of `H8` generated by its unique ordinary element `a` is predicted to be the seven elements `≠ d` (already known closed, from the non-generation proof), and predicted isomorphic to `Z_7` as a Heyting algebra via `g ↦ a, ng ↦ n, nng ↦ r, gng ↦ an, x6 ↦ rn`. This instantiates Citkin's Prop. 3.1 at the converse-refutation witness: `H8 = Z_7-core + one extra dense element`. All checks by `decide`.
4. **The tritone is the unique generator of the music lattice [K target]** (`Div12.generator_unique`). Citkin's reply (pers. comm. 2026-06-11) noted that one-generated Heyting algebras of cardinality > 5 have a *unique* generator. Kernel-checked instance at `Z_6 = Div12`: no element other than the tritone Nishimura-generates `Div12`. Method: for each non-tritone element, exhibit a proper closed predicate containing it (`nishimuraTerm_mem_of_closed`).
5. **The dense-bottom lemma [K target]** (`no_ordinary_of_least_nonzero`). If a Heyting algebra has a least nonzero element (an atom below every nonzero element), it has no ordinary elements — every nonzero element is dense. Consequence (prose, [C]-level): Citkin's prohibited algebra `P2 ≅ 2 + Z_7` (arXiv:2512.05633, §3.1) has **no** non-degenerate kernel, while `P1 ≅ Z_7` has exactly one. The prohibited family does not correlate simply with kernel-bearing — worth knowing before anyone is tempted to read structural-completeness facts through the partition.

### Falsifiers / honest outs

* If any pairwise distinctness in (1) fails abstractly, the universal threshold is false and the finite checks were artifacts — report the counterexample, do not weaken the statement silently.
* If the (2) incomparabilities are not derivable (i.e., some Heyting algebra puts `¬¬g` comparable with `g ⊔ ¬g` for ordinary `g`), the embedding claim drops to a weaker monotone-injection claim and is tagged accordingly.
* If the (3) iso fails (e.g. the core is not `Z_7`-structured), report what it is; Prop 3.1 [C] guarantees *some* `Z_{n≥6}` only.
* (4) and (5) are `decide`/short-abstract checks; failure means the statement is wrong as posed and is reported as such.

### Pre-registered [O] items (not claimed, route written down)

* **Nishimura normal form** (the big one): every element of the subalgebra generated by any `g` is a Nishimura term value in `g` — equivalently, the one-variable Heyting term algebra reduces to the ladder. Formalizing it would simultaneously (i) prove Citkin's Prop. 3.1 in full (ladder *subalgebra*, not just order-embedding, in every non-degenerate instance), and (ii) **remove the [C] from the all-n kernel law** (`hgen` discharged for every one-generated algebra), making the math anchor unconditional from the Mathlib axioms. Cost estimate: the meet/join/implication tables of the RN lattice as derived identities, by strong induction — weeks, not days. This is the highest-value remaining Lean target in the math anchor.
* **V(Z_6) primitiveness** (= the music lattice's si-logic is hereditarily structurally complete). Route: by Citkin's criterion, primitive iff omitting `P1–P5`; by Jónsson's lemma (HA congruence-distributive), s.i. members of `V(Z_6)` lie in `HS(Z_6)`, so have ≤ 6 elements; `P1 ≅ Z_7` (7 elements) and `P2 ≅ 2 + Z_7` (8) are out by cardinality. **Blocked on:** the Hasse diagrams of `P3–P5` (Fig. 1 of arXiv:2512.05633 — not recoverable from text extraction; needs the PDF figures) to confirm each is s.i. with ≥ 7 elements. If any `P_i` has ≤ 6 elements the route fails and the question is genuinely open.
