## Goal

Prove that every element of the subalgebra generated by an ordinary element `g`
in a Heyting algebra is a **Nishimura term value** in `g` — equivalently, the
one-variable term algebra reduces to the Rieger–Nishimura ladder.

## Falsifier

If some finite `Z_n` element is not a Nishimura term value in the generator,
normal form fails and Prop 3.1 [C] stays the citation.
