## Post body as posted (2026-07-12)

> I'm an independent researcher; the Lean in this project was AI-authored (Claude, working in Cursor) under my direction, so I'm posting it in this channel. Every principal claim is kernel-checked — no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, and per-theorem `#print axioms` audits reporting at most `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
>
> The project formalizes some structure theory of one-generated Heyting algebras, against Mathlib4:
>
> - **Nishimura's one-variable normal-form theorem**: every element of the subalgebra generated by an element g is the value of a Rieger–Nishimura term in g. I couldn't find this in Mathlib or elsewhere in Lean.
> - **Uniqueness of the ordinary element**: call an element *ordinary* (Citkin's term) if it is neither regular (¬¬a = a) nor dense (¬a = ⊥). In any Heyting algebra one-generated by an ordinary element, the generator is the *unique* ordinary element. The statement appears without proof in Citkin's arXiv:2512.05633; he confirmed by correspondence (cited with permission) that he knew of no written proof, so the formalization is, as far as either of us knows, the first proof written down.
> - Supporting results: all four "regions" (below a / below ¬a / below ¬¬a but not a / meeting both a and ¬a) are inhabited iff a is ordinary; any algebra with an ordinary element has ≥ 6 elements and order-embeds the six-element one-generated algebra Z₆; an 8-element counterexample showing unique-ordinary does not imply one-generated; the divisor lattice of 12 is one-generated (≅ Z₆); an exact count formula for ordinary elements of products of chains; and End(Z₆) ≅ Z₆ with every endomorphism satisfying f³ = f — a small kernel-checked instance of Ruitenburg's 1984 periodicity theorem.
>
> Source: https://github.com/thefalsework/papers — Lean in `lean/FalseWorkPapers/Examples/`, paper (with the theorem-by-Lean-name audit table in §10) in `preprints/ordinary-elements-z6/`.
>
> Two questions for the room:
>
> 1. **Has anyone formalized (or started) Ruitenburg's theorem** — for every A(p) in IPC the iteration sequence Aⁿ(p) is eventually periodic with period 2? The one-variable infrastructure here (Nishimura terms, the ladder, generated subalgebras) seems like most of the prerequisites, and I'd be glad to see it used for that.
> 2. Would any of this be **welcome in Mathlib** (e.g. the ordinary/regular/dense trichotomy for Heyting algebras, or the Rieger–Nishimura ladder)? Happy to carve out PRs if so.
>
> Corrections very welcome — if something in the axiom audit or a proof looks off, I'd rather know here than after it's on arXiv.
